What is digital marketing?

Digital marketing uses online channels to build awareness, attract valuable audiences, and encourage actions such as purchases, bookings, sign-ups, or inquiries.

It includes search engine optimization, paid search, social media marketing, content marketing, email marketing, influencer marketing, affiliate marketing, conversion optimization, and marketing analytics.
