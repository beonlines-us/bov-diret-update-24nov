Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-22

Welcome to MarketLift. We respect your privacy and explain how information may be collected, used, and protected through our website and marketing services.

1. Information We Collect

To run our services, we collect a minimal amount of information. Here’s what we collect and why:

  • Contact Information: Names, email addresses, company details, and message content submitted through forms or email.
  • Website Usage Data: Pages viewed, referring sources, device and browser information, and interactions used to improve our website.
  • Campaign Data: Advertising engagement, consent status, form submissions, and conversion events used to measure and improve campaigns.
  • Client Information: Business details and campaign data shared with us to deliver agreed marketing services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and deliver requested services.
  • Plan, operate, measure, and improve campaigns.
  • Understand website and customer experience performance.
  • Comply with legal duties and prevent misuse.
  • Communicate relevant service updates with appropriate consent.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: We may share information if you give us explicit permission to do so.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to a valid legal request.
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party companies for services like website hosting or analytics. These providers only have access to the information necessary to perform their functions and are contractually obligated to protect it.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your personal information. You have the right to:

  • Access and Update: You can access and update your account information through your profile settings (if applicable).
  • Data Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us.
  • Clear Local Data: You can control cookies and locally stored preferences through your browser settings.
  • Opt-Out of Communications: You can unsubscribe from any promotional emails we might send in the future.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed at business audiences and are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact privacy@marketlift.com.
