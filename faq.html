FAQ

Answers to common questions about digital marketing and MarketLift.

What is digital marketing?

Digital marketing uses online channels to reach audiences, build brand awareness, generate demand, and drive measurable customer actions.

Which types of marketing should we use?

The right mix depends on your audience, offer, sales cycle, geography, budget, and goals. We usually evaluate search, social, content, email, paid media, and conversion optimization together.

How is a strategy developed?

We combine audience research, competitor insights, channel analysis, measurement review, and your commercial objectives into a prioritized roadmap.

How quickly can campaigns launch?

Simple campaigns may launch within weeks. Research-heavy branding, technical SEO, tracking, and integrated programs typically need a longer setup period.

How do you measure success?

We agree on primary outcomes before launch, such as qualified leads, sales, bookings, revenue, retention, or campaign efficiency.

Do you manage paid advertising?

Yes. We plan, create, launch, test, optimize, and report on search, social, and display campaigns.

Can you improve existing campaigns?

Yes. We audit targeting, creative, landing pages, tracking, budgets, bidding, and measurement before recommending improvements.

Do you provide content creation?

We can develop articles, landing pages, campaign copy, email sequences, social creative, and conversion-focused messaging.

What makes SEO effective?

Effective SEO combines technical health, relevant content, useful structure, authority, clear internal links, and a good experience across devices.

How often should reports be reviewed?

Campaign pace varies. Many programs use weekly optimization reviews and monthly strategy reports, with executive summaries for stakeholders.

How do you protect customer data?

We use consent-aware collection, access controls, secure tools, minimal data retention, and clear privacy practices.

How do we get started?

Use the contact form or email us with your goals, current channels, target timeline, and approximate budget.
